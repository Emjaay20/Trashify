## Solution

Trashify coordinates household waste pickups and sanitation follow-up. Residents book a pickup, approved collectors confirm collection with the resident's PIN, and estate or municipal teams review overdue pickups and sanitation reports by postcode district.

## Postcode use

ResolveGrid is our team's own postcode layer. In this pilot, it calls NIPOST's official disassembly and state, LGA, district and area reference endpoints. Trashify labels these results “Postcode structure verified with NIPOST. Building verification pending live access.” The result validates syntax and administrative membership. Building existence is pending access. Collector eligibility and staff access are scoped by digital postcode district.

The test key supports structure and reference calls. Building lookup and search currently do not return usable records. CAC registration, NIPOST business verification, live keys and Postcode Autofill are planned next steps. All public demonstrations use test data.

## Public health and AI

The public-health lead will define pilot sites, baseline measurements and follow-up checks. Current measures include on-time collection, still-overdue pickups, median hours to collect and report closure. Measuring illness outcomes would require a separate study.

Optional AI suggests a report category using allowlisted sanitation signals and chooses the focus of summaries made from district aggregates. Names, phone numbers, addresses and building codes stay out of model inputs. Deterministic fallbacks work without a model key. AI assistance is labelled and users review report suggestions.
