# Trashify public-health pilot

Trashify began with its originator's public-health idea. She can lead the choice of pilot sites, the definition of a missed pickup, the interpretation of sanitation reports and the evaluation design. Review these definitions with her before recruiting households.

## What the product measures

| Measure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Pickups due | Trashify bookings in the workspace whose collection window ended in the selected 30- or 90-day period. |
| On-time collection | Collections recorded by the end of the pickup window divided by all pickups due. The dashboard shows numerator and denominator. |
| Still overdue | Pickups due that have no recorded completion when the dashboard is viewed. A collection completed late stays outside the on-time count. |
| Time to collect | Median hours from booking to recorded completion among completed pickups in the due-window cohort. |
| Sanitation reports | Structured reports of overflow, open dumping, blocked drains, observed insects or rodents around waste, or a missed scheduled pickup. One report per issue type per pickup. |
| Report closure | A service team records when it has addressed the issue. The report queue displays open reports across all dates. Period counts include reports created during that period. |

The municipal dashboard is scoped to authorized postcode districts. Estate managers see enrolled households only. The downloadable CSV contains service metrics by postcode district and omits zones with fewer than five due pickups. Median wait is omitted when fewer than five pickups were completed. Review disclosure risk before sharing an export, especially when comparing overlapping periods or small estates.

## How to evaluate the pilot

1. Define a baseline period and use the same collection windows and reporting categories during the pilot. If feasible, compare with similar areas that have not yet adopted Trashify.
2. Report uptake, missing data and the number of participating households separately. Trashify's zone comparisons describe booked pickups. Estimating neighbourhood coverage requires an independent denominator such as the number of eligible households.
3. Track why pickups were missed and validate resident observations with service teams or structured site inspections. A closed report records the team's action. Independent checks can verify whether the problem recurred.
4. Decide in advance which operational measures would justify expanding the pilot. If the work becomes human-participant research or collects health outcomes, agree the protocol, ethics review, permissions and retention with the responsible institution before collecting those data.

The app collects operational indicators and resident observations. A change in these figures alone cannot establish a reduction in illness. Keep clinical data, diagnoses and names of people affected out of the reporting workflow.

The [World Health Organization's solid waste and health overview](https://www.who.int/news/item/16-12-2025-who-highlights-health-risks-and-opportunities-in-the-global-waste-crisis) describes how uncontrolled waste can create environmental exposures and breeding grounds for pests.
